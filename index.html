Appt Nanny support

Appt Nanny reminds you of your appointments and daily tasks out loud, tells you when to leave, and lets you add things by speaking. It is made by FaberWire LLC.

Get help

Email us at faberwirellc@gmail.com. Tell us what you tapped or said and what happened, and which phone you have. We answer every email.

You can also send feedback from inside the app, in Settings. It opens your email app with the details filled in.

Emergency

Appt Nanny's help word texts or emails your emergency contact and opens your phone's call screen. You still press Call. It does not call emergency services by itself.

Privacy

Your appointments and reminders stay on your phone. No ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
