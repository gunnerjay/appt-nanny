Appt Nanny privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Appt Nanny (also shown as Appointment Nanny) is made by FaberWire LLC.

The short version

Appt Nanny keeps your appointments, reminders and settings on your phone. We have no account for you, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never sell or rent anything about you. Something leaves your phone only when you send it, or when a feature you turned on needs it, as listed below.

What stays on your phone

  • Your appointments, reminders, home address, saved places, the contacts you pick, your help word and your settings.
  • What you say to the app. Speech is turned into words on the phone itself and is not sent anywhere.
  • Your calendar, if you let the app read it to offer its appointments.
  • Your location, used to work out when to leave and for directions.

We cannot see any of this. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone, and when

  • Emergency. When you say your help word, the app sends your emergency contact a message with where you are and a map link: a text message on Android, an email from your own Gmail on iPhone. It goes only to the contact you picked.
  • Sharing your schedule. When you share your day, it goes to the person you pick, through your own messaging or email app.
  • Finding places. When you search for a nearby place, the words you search for and your general area are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search service to find matches. Their policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.
  • Addresses and directions. To turn your location into an address, the app uses your phone's own map service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone). Directions open in Google Maps or Apple Maps.
  • Feedback and crash reports. Only if you choose to send them. They open in your own email app, so you see everything before it goes to us at faberwirellc@gmail.com.
  • Sync (Android, optional). Off unless you connect the app to a server of your own in Settings. Your schedule then goes to that server, which you control, not to us.

Signing in with Google

To send emergency emails from your Gmail, the app asks Google for one permission: to send email for you. It does not read, keep or delete your email, and the sign-in stays on your phone. You can sign out any time in the app's Emergency contact settings, or remove the app from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Appt Nanny's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we keep

Only the emails you send us (feedback, crash reports, questions), in our inbox, to answer you and fix problems. Ask us and we will delete them.

Children

Appt Nanny is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
